Compared with doing it yourself or hiring a large firm

Doing it yourself costs nothing up front and takes weeks a founder rarely has. It also tends to miss sources outside your own field, such as FDA records or patents.

Large firms bring big teams and primary research, priced and scoped for large companies. You often work with someone other than the person doing the research.

ZKK is two engineers. The people on your call are the people doing the work, on a fixed price from $1,999 to $7,499.

Questions

Common questions

Can't ChatGPT or another AI tool do market research?
It can draft a first pass quickly. It also states numbers it cannot source, mixes up similar companies and products, and cannot tell you which figure an investor will challenge. Our work uses AI where it saves time, and a person checks every number against its source.
Do you use AI in your research?
Yes, for searching and drafting. Nothing reaches the report until one of us has read the source and confirmed the figure.
Is ZKK cheaper than a large consulting firm?
Usually. We are two engineers on fixed prices from $1,999 to $7,499, with no account managers or overhead in between.
What if I only need something quick?
Tell us on the free call. If a short piece of work or your own research is enough, we will say so.
