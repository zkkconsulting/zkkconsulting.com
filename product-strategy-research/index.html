Strategy

Product strategy research

Product strategy research is research-supported analysis for product and go-to-market decisions. ZKK offers it in the $3,499 Comprehensive package, which adds pricing and go-to-market analysis, scenario analysis around key assumptions, and a decision-ready synthesis of findings.

Where it fits

Strategy research options

  • Comprehensive package, $3,499. Includes expanded commercial and competitive analysis, scenario analysis around key assumptions, decision-ready synthesis of research findings, and two add-ons.
  • Adjacent Market Screen, $250. A structured screen of two or three adjacent markets, compared on rough size, competition, entry considerations, and fit.
  • Scenario & Sensitivity Modeling, from $499. An editable model that tests the assumptions most likely to affect the outcome.
  • Recurring Research Brief, quoted. A recurring brief on the cadence you choose, covering competitor moves, market shifts, regulatory developments, or another defined question.
Limits

What strategic considerations are

Where a package includes strategic considerations or recommendations, those are research-supported options and trade-offs drawn from the sources and assumptions in the deliverable. They are not guarantees of outcome and they are not legal, financial, medical, or other professional advice. Every decision remains yours.
