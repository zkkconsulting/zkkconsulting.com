How It Works

What happens after you reach out

Timelines are set during scoping based on the scope of the work and your deadlines.

01
Discovery call

A no-cost conversation about your business, market, and the decision the research should inform.

02
Scope & agreement

We confirm deliverables, selected add-ons, price, and timeline, then send a Statement of Work.

03
Research

Market, competitive, technical, and regulatory research, with sources and assumptions documented throughout.

04
Delivery & walkthrough

A finished, sourced deliverable you can circulate, followed by a live walkthrough of the findings and your questions.
