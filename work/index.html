SAMPLE PROJECT MEDICAL DEVICES
Market, patent, and commercial research for wrist-worn fall detection
COMPREHENSIVE PACKAGE SAMPLE + PATENT LANDSCAPE RESEARCH
Question

An internal hardware program needs a grounded view of its United States market, competitive and patent context, and the commercial assumptions behind a five-year outlook.

Research

Market sizing, competitor, buyer, patent, regulatory and reimbursement, supply-chain, pricing, and channel research, paired with an editable five-year commercial model.

Result

A sourced report and an editable Excel model with base, downside, and upside cases. Built from ZKK’s own internal hardware program, not client work. Model projections are illustrative, not forecasts.
