Why hire

Why hire a consultant instead of doing it yourself?

You can do market research yourself, and AI tools make a first pass faster. What a consultant adds is time and checking: every number is traced to a source, assumptions are written down, and the report holds up when an investor asks where a figure came from.

We compare the options honestly in research consultant vs doing it yourself or with AI tools.

Process

How an engagement works

  1. Free call. We talk through the decision you face and what research would answer it.
  2. Statement of work. Deliverables, a fixed price and a timeline, agreed before we start.
  3. Research. Sources and assumptions are recorded as we go.
  4. Delivery. A walkthrough call, then one round of revisions.

Payment is 50% to start and 50% on completion. A mutual NDA is available. Read the sample reports to see the format first.

Questions

Common questions

How much does a market research consultant cost for a startup?
ZKK's packages are fixed-price: $2,499 for Launchpad, $4,499 for Growth and $7,499 for Comprehensive. Patent Landscape research is $1,999. Payment is 50% to start and 50% on completion.
Do you work with pre-revenue startups?
Yes. Many clients are early teams checking a market before they build, pitch or apply for a grant.
Will the research work in a pitch deck?
Yes. Growth includes TAM, SAM and SOM with sources, and the competitor findings can feed your competitor slide.
Do you only work with medical device startups?
No. We focus on engineering-led products such as devices, hardware and electronics, but we work with clients of any size, in any industry and location.
Is this investment or legal advice?
No. It is business research. It is not legal, regulatory, medical, tax or investment advice.
