Questions to ask before hiring a market research consultant

Seven questions for the first call, and the red flags to walk away from.

Book a free consultationSee pricing
Short answer

Seven questions that separate good research from expensive guesswork

Last updated November 18, 2026

Most of the risk in hiring a research consultant is in what you did not ask before signing. These questions take ten minutes on a first call and tell you most of what you need.

The questions

Ask these before you sign

  1. Can I see a sample report? If there is nothing to show, you are buying a promise.
  2. Will every number cite a source I can open? Anything else is hard to defend in front of an investor.
  3. Who does the work? The person on the sales call is not always the person doing the research.
  4. Is the price fixed, and what does it include? Get the deliverables and revisions in writing.
  5. What is out of scope? A good consultant is clear about what they will not answer, such as legal or regulatory opinions.
  6. Do you know my field? Device, software and consumer markets use very different sources.
  7. What happens after delivery? Ask whether there is a walkthrough and a round of changes.
Red flags

Signs to walk away

  • Guaranteed outcomes, such as funding or regulatory clearance.
  • A market size quoted before they know your product.
  • Hourly billing with no estimate or cap.
  • No written scope.

Questions

Common questions

How many consultants should I talk to?
Two or three is usually enough to compare samples, scope and price.
Should I sign an NDA first?
For a first call, usually not needed. Before sharing confidential product details, ask for a mutual NDA. ZKK offers one.
What should I prepare for the first call?
The decision you face, your deadline, and anything you already know about competitors or customers.
