Overview

Research for better business and product decisions

ZKK Consulting is a market research and business growth consulting firm. We help companies make better business and product-development decisions through technical, financial and competitive research.

Most of our work answers one of a few questions: how big is the market, who are we up against, what is already patented, and what do the numbers look like over the next five years. Every engagement starts with a free consultation, and we shape a fixed-price package around your question.

We work with startups, medical device teams and companies of any size, in any industry and location. Our deepest experience is in medical devices, electronics and regulated hardware.

Services

What we research

Market sizing

TAM, SAM and SOM estimates built bottom-up where data allows, with every input sourced or stated as an assumption. Customer, buyer and channel research shows who buys and how, and adjacent markets are screened where they change the picture. See market sizing for startups.

Competitor analysis

Who you are up against, how they position and price, and where the gaps are. For device teams this includes how your product might differ from what is already on the market. See medical device competitive landscape analysis.

Patent landscapes

A view of public patent activity around your technology, organized so your team and your patent counsel can see who is filing what. Business research only, with no legal conclusions. See patent landscape research and medical device patent landscapes.

Regulation, standards and supply chains

FDA and FCC pathways, relevant standards and supply chain context from public sources, so you know what stands between a prototype and a market. This is research, not regulatory advice.

Financial models and investor materials

Five-year financial models in editable Excel, go-to-market analysis and investor-ready materials, including pitch deck review. These come with the larger packages and bundles on the services page.

Who it is for

Built for startups, open to any team

  • Startups preparing a raise, a pitch deck or a first product decision.
  • Medical device and hardware teams weighing a concept, a market or a regulatory path.
  • Established companies exploring a new product line, market or competitor.

Our team is engineering-led, which helps when the product is technical. Meet us on the team page, or read more about ZKK Consulting.

How it works

From first call to final report

  1. Book a free consultation and tell us the decision you are facing.
  2. We scope a package around that question and quote a fixed price.
  3. We research, source every figure and deliver a written report.
  4. You review it, and one round of revisions is included.

Samples of our report format are on the samples page, and plain-language guides explain the methods. Prices and package contents are on services and pricing.

We provide business research and analysis. It is not legal, regulatory or investment advice. See our disclaimers.
