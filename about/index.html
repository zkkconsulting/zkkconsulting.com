About

About ZKK Consulting

ZKK Consulting LLC is a two-person Texas consulting firm for engineering-led products. It provides fixed-price market research, patent landscape research, competitive pricing analysis and strategy research for startups and medical device teams. We work with clients of any size, in any industry and location.

Who We Are

A two-person firm

We are a two-person firm. You work directly with the people doing the research, without an account layer between you and the work.

  • Zolomon Kaliser, Founder: market analysis, financial modeling, and medtech research.
  • Jad Haddad, Co-Founder: patent, technical, and product-market research.

ZKK Consulting LLC is a Texas limited liability company, co-founded by Zolomon Kaliser and Jad Haddad. See the full research team page for backgrounds.

Why ZKK

Research capacity without the full-time overhead

Clear, sourced research for the decisions in front of you, without building an internal team or retaining a large consulting firm.

Technical and commercial research in one team

We combine engineering and finance backgrounds to connect technical details, regulatory context, and market analysis in the same engagement.

Clear scope, clear price

Every engagement starts with agreed deliverables, a clear price, and a timeline set during scoping. No hourly billing.

Traceable, not a black box

Every figure is sourced and every estimate includes its assumptions, so your team, board, and investors can review the work instead of taking it on faith.

How It Works

From question to answer
