Hardware design & compliance

Fall-detection wearable, engineered around FCC testing

Originated and led the technical design of a wrist-worn fall-detection wearable: PCB through revision 3.0.0, bench prototype and full component specification.

  • Moved the IMU to the LSM6DSV16XTR for lower power and on-chip machine learning.
  • Chose a pre-certified ESP32-C3 Mini module, avoiding FCC Part 15C intentional-radiator testing.
  • Built a companion app that retrains the fall-detection model on each user’s gait.
  • Ran prior-art research to shape the IP strategy.
