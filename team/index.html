About

Market, patent and technical research.

Zolomon Kaliser founded ZKK Consulting to close the gap between what a medical technology can do and what it can earn. With a Master of Engineering in Biomedical Engineering and a degree in Finance, he tests the engineering claims and the financial model with the same rigor.

He has led feasibility, market and competitive studies on remote patient monitoring, post-transplant care and reusable surgical devices, and rebuilt a wearable’s bill of materials to cut unit component cost by 71%. His models project revenue, COGS and operating expenses, with every input traced to a source.

About

Market, patent and technical research.

Jad Haddad co-founded ZKK Consulting to ground commercial decisions in engineering reality. He takes products apart to see how they are designed, sourced and certified, and what that means for cost, risk and time to market.

He led the hardware architecture of a health-monitoring wearable through its v3.0.0 PCB, choosing a pre-certified radio module that avoided FCC intentional-radiator testing. At Oncor Electric Delivery he studied fault response and capacitor-bank protection on transmission tied to the Comanche Peak Nuclear Power Plant; his animations became the team’s training standard.
