Why us

Researchers who read the engineering

Device markets turn on technical details: sensor type, form factor, power, clearance history. ZKK's co-founders combine engineering and finance training, so the research reads product documentation and patents the way an engineer would. Meet them on the team page.

Location

Texas-based, working anywhere

ZKK Consulting LLC is a Texas limited liability company. We work with device teams across Texas and in any other state or country, by video call and shared documents. Every engagement has a fixed price, a written statement of work, a 50% deposit to start and 50% on completion. A mutual NDA is available.
