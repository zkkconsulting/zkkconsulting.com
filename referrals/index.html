Referral program

Know a team that needs research? Get 15% off your next package

Introduce us. When they sign and pay for their first engagement, your next package is 15% off.

Make an introductionWhat to refer them to

How it works

  1. Introduce us. Email the introduction, or have them mention your name, before their Statement of Work is signed.
  2. They engage us. We scope their work like any other. Your reward activates once they pay in full.
  3. You save 15%. Apply it to your next package. No cap on how many teams you introduce.

Program terms

  • The 15% applies to one future package or item, not an entire multi-item order.
  • It activates after the referred client's initial and final invoices are paid in full.
  • It expires 12 months after it is issued.
  • Bundles receive a maximum referral discount of 10%.
  • Rewards are not transferable, cannot be exchanged for cash, and cannot be combined on the same item.
  • ZKK Consulting LLC may modify or end the program at any time. Issued rewards stay valid until they expire.
