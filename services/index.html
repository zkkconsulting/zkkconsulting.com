Standalone Service

Patent Landscape Research

Organize publicly available patent information around your technology before committing significant engineering time or capital. Available as a standalone service or bundled with market research.

What the service includes

  • Research across the technology area and competitive landscape defined during scoping
  • Review and comparison of up to 15 identified patents
  • Summary and comparison matrix of technical features, ownership and status information, and relevant commercial context
  • Side-by-side view of apparent technical overlaps and distinctions, based on publicly available information
  • Competitive patent activity, including filing patterns and areas of concentration
  • Business and market context for discussion with qualified patent counsel
  • Written report with the patent list, sources, and documented search approach

Important: Business and market research only. ZKK Consulting does not provide legal advice or legal opinions. This work does not determine patentability, freedom to operate, infringement, or validity. Consult qualified patent counsel for legal conclusions.

How pricing is measured

  • Pricing is based on the original document you send us, not the length of the reformatted version. If a redesign increases the page count, your price does not increase.
  • We consider word count alongside page count, so a dense document with very small type is priced more fairly than page count alone would allow.
  • For unusually dense or complex files, or when the initial scope is inaccurate, we will provide an adjusted quote before work begins.

Engagement Terms

How the work is structured

Payment terms

Each engagement has a defined scope and agreed price. After scoping, we send a Statement of Work and a Stripe invoice for the 50% deposit. Work begins once both are complete. The remaining 50% is invoiced on completion. The same terms apply to packages, standalone services, bundles, and custom scopes.

Timelines

Timelines are set during scoping and included in the Statement of Work. They depend on research depth, selected add-ons, access to materials, and your deadlines. If you are working toward a fixed date, tell us early so we can confirm what is realistic.

One round of revisions

Each engagement includes one round of revisions. Within five business days of delivery, send one consolidated set of feedback. We will refine the deliverable so it matches the agreed scope.

Confidential by default

Your business information and materials remain private and are not reused, sold, or shared. We are happy to sign a mutual NDA before you share proprietary information. Where appropriate, limited non-solicitation or exclusivity terms can be discussed and included in the Statement of Work.

What you receive

Written analysis with sources and assumptions documented throughout, plus any models, matrices, or visuals included in scope. Models are delivered in editable files, not locked exports, so your team can continue using them after the engagement closes.

Scope of our work

We provide research and analysis, including sourced findings, assumptions, estimates, and decision-relevant context. We do not provide legal, regulatory, medical, tax, accounting, or investment advice, and our deliverables are not a substitute for those services.
