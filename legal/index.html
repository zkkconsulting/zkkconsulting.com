01 Scope of services and no professional advice

Reports, models, matrices, briefs, presentations, visuals, and other deliverables provided by ZKK Consulting LLC are business research and analysis only. We present sources, assumptions, findings, estimates, and possible considerations. Our deliverables are not legal, regulatory, medical, clinical, tax, accounting, or investment advice, and nothing on this site or in any deliverable should be relied on as such.

Where a deliverable includes strategic considerations or recommendations, those are research-supported options and trade-offs drawn from the sources and assumptions stated in that deliverable. They are not guarantees or predictions of outcome, and they are not a substitute for advice from a qualified licensed professional.

Nothing in any deliverable is a guarantee or prediction of funding, regulatory clearance or approval, certification, patent grant or enforceability, coverage, reimbursement, market acceptance, or financial results. Clients remain solely responsible for their own regulatory, legal, clinical, commercial, and business decisions and should obtain advice from qualified licensed professionals before acting.

02 Patent landscape research

Patent Landscape Research is strategic business and market research only. ZKK Consulting LLC is not a law firm, does not practice before the United States Patent and Trademark Office or any other patent office, and does not provide legal advice.

A patent landscape deliverable is not a legal patentability opinion, not a validity opinion, and not a formal Freedom-to-Operate opinion. We do not determine or opine on infringement, non-infringement, patent validity, enforceability, or claim construction. Functional overlaps, competitive considerations, and potential business risks identified in a deliverable are business observations offered for discussion, not legal conclusions.

Patent research reflects the databases and published records available to us as of the report date. Applications may be unpublished, records may be incomplete, and the field changes continuously. Clients should consult qualified patent counsel for legal conclusions and determinations before relying on any patent-related finding.

03 Regulatory and standards research

Research covering FDA, FCC, and other regulatory or standards frameworks describes what publicly available regulations, guidance documents, standards, and databases say as of the report date. It is offered as research and technical consulting support.

ZKK Consulting LLC does not provide legal advice, does not issue regulatory opinions or determinations, does not certify products, and does not represent clients in regulatory, enforcement, or legal proceedings. We are not your regulatory agent or legal representative. Classification, clearance, certification, and compliance determinations rest with the relevant agencies and accredited bodies. Where a legal determination or representation is required, qualified counsel or an appropriately qualified regulatory professional should be retained.

Reimbursement, coverage, and coding research reflects publicly available sources as of the report date and is not billing or coding guidance. Coverage policies, code assignments, and payment rates change and vary by payer, site of service, and jurisdiction.

08 Referral program terms

The referral program described on this site offers a discount on a future engagement to a person or company who introduces a new client to ZKK Consulting LLC. The core terms are:

  • The 15% reward applies to one future package or item, not to an entire multi-item order.
  • The reward activates only after the referred client's initial and final invoices are paid in full.
  • The reward expires 12 months after issuance.
  • Bundles receive a maximum referral discount of 10%, in place of the 15%.
  • Rewards are not transferable, have no cash value, and may not be combined with another referral reward on the same item.
  • The referral must be attributable to the referrer before the referred client's Statement of Work is signed.

ZKK Consulting LLC may modify or discontinue the program at any time. Rewards already issued remain valid through their stated expiration date. Full details are on the referral program page.
